PM Review — Risks, Analytics & Safeguards
Status: Complete  ·  February 2026
This section documents six product-level improvements identified during senior PM review. All items are incorporated into the build plan before development begins.

PM.1  MVP Scope Revision — WhatsApp First

PM.2  Monitoring Stack
Monitoring is non-optional from Day 1. A server crash with no alerting means hours of undetected downtime for a 24x7 client-facing system.


PM.3  Offer Expiry Intelligence
Without expiry filtering, the Client Assistant Bot will retrieve and present expired offers as current. This directly damages client trust.


PM.4  Abuse Protection & Prompt Injection Guard
The Client Assistant Bot receives free-text input from unknown clients. Without safeguards, prompt injection attacks can expose system information or produce off-brand responses.


PM.5  Analytics KPIs
Delivery tracking (sent/delivered/read/failed) is already in the schema. The following product-level KPIs are added to the analytics view in the proprietor dashboard.


PM.6  Owner Onboarding Flow
The system is only as effective as the owner's ability to use it correctly. An onboarding flow must be designed before launch so the proprietor understands how to operate the system from Day 1.


PM.8  Scale Trigger Plan
The single-server architecture is sufficient for Phase 1. The following table defines the exact thresholds at which infrastructure must be upgraded. Monitoring metrics from Sentry and UptimeRobot will surface these triggers automatically.


PM.9  Conversation Quality Control
Bot resolution rate and escalation rate measure quantity. Quality control measures whether the responses the bot does give are actually correct and useful. Without this, the bot can silently degrade without the owner noticing.


PM.10  Revenue & Pricing Model
Even if this system is built for a single business initially, defining the pricing model now ensures the architecture supports multi-tenancy and commercial expansion without redesign.



PM.11  Tenant Isolation Design
Multi-tenancy requires strict data isolation between business owners. Without enforcement, one tenant can inadvertently access another tenant's clients, broadcasts, or knowledge base. This must be built in from the first migration.


PM.12  WhatsApp Quality Rating Safeguards
The entire Phase 1 client communication channel depends on a single WhatsApp Business number. Meta assigns a quality rating to every number based on client engagement and block rates. A degraded rating reduces messaging limits and can result in a temporary or permanent ban. Protecting this number is a critical operational priority.
